# Taiwan rail package 2025 — official rebuild

`tw-2025.json` is rebuilt from official Taiwan government/open-operator data.
It uses the same `compact-v1` schema as `jp-2025.json` and contains 38
passenger railway, metro, and light-rail display lines.

The 2025 package name is retained for compatibility with the app's country
package selector. Version `2025.4.0` is a clean official-data rebuild; it does
not inherit station order, station grouping, or geometry from an older Taiwan
package. Version `2025.4.1` corrects the routing over that same data: the
Dulishan spiral, the Alishan zigzag reversal tails, and the Zhushan terminal
loop are kept via official-track via points (interval lengths now match the
AFR 營業里程 table); station anchors are weighted so terminals are no longer
shaved and interval boundaries sit at the stations; coarse TRA shape chords
are subdivided so they only bridge genuine NLSC breaks (fixing a 9 km
double-back near 豐田 on the Taitung line); and the Taipei GIS preference no
longer rewards longer detours.

Version `2025.5.0` grooms that routed geometry for map display without changing
its official-data authority. Every station marker is now the exact on-line
projection used as the shared boundary of its two adjacent intervals. A train
therefore follows the railway through the station instead of turning into and
back out of a platform throat. Lines grouped under the same physical station
may retain separate on-line points when their platforms or alignments differ.
Short source-node sawteeth are relaxed, redundant vertices are simplified,
and ordinary corners are resampled as bounded quadratic curves; genuine
Alishan switchbacks remain sharp. Same-operator route terminals are welded
only over their own scoped official geometry, with station-throat retraces
removed before output.

Version `2025.5.1` corrects the Alishan Forest Railway to its real alignment,
verified interval-by-interval against the official operating-mileage table
(afrch.forest.gov.tw) and the 2024 tunnel-42 reopening. The NLSC current
layer is now the preferred routing source for AFR (the MOA detail file keeps
its gap-bridging role — the zigzag tails exist only there), dense via points
sampled along the chained NLSC centreline pin the full Dulishan spiral and
its figure-eight exit, symmetric sub-100 m out-and-back routing artefacts are
trimmed while genuine switchback tails stay, and AFR lines groom with lighter
smoothing to preserve their tight curve radii. Every interval now sits within
~0.2 km of the official table (spiral: 4.18/3.92 km vs official 4.10/4.00).

Version `2025.5.2` adds a minimum corner radius. The corner-rounding pass
fillets between two neighbouring vertices, so it declines wherever the mixed
TDX/NLSC digitising puts vertices a few metres apart, and those corners kept
whatever angle the source left — on the forest lines the line swung harder
over 20 m than the real 762 mm alignment can, which is a digitising artefact,
not track. Below about z13 a corner that tight is far narrower than one pixel,
and a sub-pixel curve reads as a barb rather than a curve. Corners tighter
than a 40 m radius, measured over an arc-length window rather than between
adjacent vertices, are now relaxed until they clear it, anchored within 3 m of
where the rest of the grooming left them. Reversal cusps are exempt: all four
阿里山 zigzag tails survive untouched. Network-wide this removes 77 of 179
sub-40 m corners (metro lines reach zero) for 46 m of length across 1796 km,
and the official-geometry comparison is unchanged at a 17.65 m maximum.

## Data authorities

- Ministry of Transportation TDX/PTX rail APIs
  - Official Shape, Station, and StationOfLine responses for Taiwan High Speed
    Rail, Taiwan Railways, Taipei Metro, New Taipei Metro, Taoyuan Airport MRT,
    Taichung Metro, Kaohsiung Metro, Danhai LRT, Ankeng LRT, and Kaohsiung LRT.
  - API documentation: <https://tdx.transportdata.tw/api-service/swagger/basic/5fa88b0c-120b-43f1-b188-c379ddb2593d>
- Ministry of the Interior, National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (NLSC)
  - Railway centerlines: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/73220>
  - Railway stations: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/73226>
  - Metro centerlines: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/73222>
  - Light-rail centerlines: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/73229>
  - Light-rail stations: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/73230>
- Ministry of Agriculture, Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Heritage Office
  - Detailed Alishan/ Zhushan SHP: <https://data.gov.tw/dataset/38129>
  - Official station sequence and mileage: <https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/>
- Taipei City Department of Rapid Transit Systems
  - Taipei metropolitan MRT network GIS JSON:
    <https://data.taipei/dataset/detail?id=afccd2ac-75b1-4362-9099-45983e332776>

All sources above are public official data. Their applicable license is the
Government Open Data License, version 1, or the publishing authority's stated
open-data terms.

## Geometry method

TDX/PTX supplies official station membership, station order, names, English
names, coordinates, and WGS84 route Shapes. NLSC centerlines are combined with
the matching TDX Shapes to resolve official-record topology breaks and retain
detailed station-area geometry. The source graph is strictly isolated by the
official `LineID`, `RAILNAME`, `MRTCODE`, or `LRTCODE`; unrelated tracks are
never placed in a line's routing graph. Taipei City's detailed MRT GIS is
preferred for the matching Taipei line curves. The Ministry of Agriculture
detailed SHP closes the NLSC Zhushan topology gap; the NLSC layer supplies the
Alishan approach.

Each logical display line is built from a blank data structure. The generator:

1. selects the official ordered station list;
2. identifies an official connected component covering every station;
3. uses a dynamic program over official platform/track candidates so adjacent
   station intervals share the same official graph node; anchor offsets are
   weighted (3x) so shaving track past a station never pays for itself;
4. routes every interval through official graph edges, forced through
   official-track via points where the published alignment reverses or loops
   (the Dulishan spiral, the Alishan switchback tails, the Zhushan terminal
   loop);
5. rejects disconnected paths, implausible shortcuts/detours, and any
   synthetic connector;
6. projects every display station onto its routed line, re-cuts the adjacent
   intervals at that byte-identical coordinate, and keeps platform-separated
   lines at separate coordinates within the shared station group;
7. trims short in-and-out platform spurs, applies capped metre-scale relaxation
   and simplification, rounds ordinary corners, and holds every remaining
   corner to a 40 m minimum radius measured over an arc-length window — all
   while retaining deliberate forest-rail reversal cusps;
8. welds compatible same-operator route terminals over scoped official
   geometry and removes any tiny terminal retrace created by that weld;
9. subdivides sparse official edges collinearly — graph input at 200 m (TRA
   shapes at 100 m, so a coarse chord can bridge an NLSC break without being
   drawn whole) and output segments at 200 m — without changing the published
   alignment, so no compact segment is a two-coordinate chord;
10. compares every generated vertex and every edge midpoint with that line's
   scoped official source geometry and fails above a 20 m tolerance.

The output audit requires 38 lines, continuous adjacent segments, zero
two-point segments, zero synthetic connectors, and no output coordinate edge
of 200 m or longer. The package records the comparison result for each line in
`geometrySource.officialGeometryComparison.byLine`. OpenStreetMap input and
fallback count are both zero.

## Rebuild

Download fresh official snapshots to a temporary directory:

```sh
cd app
python3 scripts/download-taiwan-rail-official.py \
  --output-dir /private/tmp/tw-rail-official
```

Then perform the clean build:

```sh
python3 scripts/build-taiwan-rail-package.py \
  --source-dir /private/tmp/tw-rail-official \
  --alishan-detail /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/alishan_official_11001.zip \
  --alishan-rail /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/tw_rail_open.zip \
  --alishan-stations /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/tw_rail_station_open.zip \
  --nlsc-mrt /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/tw_mrt_open.zip \
  --nlsc-lrt /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/tw_lrt_open.zip \
  --nlsc-lrt-stations /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/tw_lrt_station_open.zip \
  --taipei-metro /private/tmp/tw-rail-official/taipei_metro_official.json
```

That same command also writes the app's Taiwan solver datasets,
`data/rail-sections-tw.json` and `data/stations-tw.json`. They are the
country's half of the schema the route solver, the station index, and the
mileage statistics read for every country — `line_name`, `operator`,
`institution_type_code`, `railway_class_code` on sections, and
`station_name`, `n02_station_code` (the official TDX StationUID),
`n02_group_code`, `display_point` on stations; Japan supplies the same facts
through the historical `N02_*` names. They are emitted here rather than
derived from `tw-2025.json` because only this build still holds the per-line
StationUID (the package carries just the shared station group id), and because
sharing the routed geometry guarantees a solved route lies on the drawn line.
The two countries' datasets are never loaded together: same-named stations
exist in both (松山, 板橋, 岡山 …).

Build the separate four-language Taiwan station-name table from the same
official snapshots:

```sh
python3 scripts/build-taiwan-station-readings.py \
  --source-dir /private/tmp/tw-rail-official
```

`StationName.Zh_tw`, `.En`, and `.Ja` are copied from TDX/PTX without
inventing translations. Missing English or Japanese values remain empty.
TDX/PTX currently publishes no Simplified Chinese station-name field, so
`zh_Hans` uses the official field when one appears and otherwise converts the
official Traditional Chinese name directly to Simplified Chinese. NLSC/AFR
display-only stations retain their official Traditional name; unavailable
translations remain empty.

The generated package records a SHA-256 fingerprint for every downloaded
input. The large source snapshots are not committed. The deterministic gzip
sidecar is generated locally for the server/static build workflow.
